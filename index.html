Privacy Policy for Block Puzzle Game

Last Updated: June 4, 2026

Protecting your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how the "Block Puzzle Game" application handles user data.

Information We Collect

Personal Information: We want to assure you that the "Block Puzzle Game" application does not collect, track, or store any personally identifiable information from you.

Game Progress and Data: All game data, high scores, and puzzle progress are generated, processed, and stored locally on your device only. We do not access, transmit, or store your gameplay data on external servers.

Advertising Information (Google AdMob): This application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When advertisements are shown, AdMob may automatically collect and use certain non-personal information about your device (such as device model, OS version, and advertising ID) and your activity within the application. This information is solely governed by the Google AdMob privacy policy, which can be found at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

How We Use Your Information

  • Feature Operation: The application's features, including gameplay and score tracking, operate entirely on your device without the need to transfer or store any personal data on our end.
  • Advertising: The information collected by AdMob is used to provide relevant advertisements and improve their advertising services. You can learn more about how Google uses this data in the Google Privacy Policy, which can be found at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Sharing of Your Information

  • Advertising Partners (AdMob): We do not share any personal information with AdMob. However, please be aware that AdMob may collect and handle data in accordance with their own privacy policy linked above.

Data Security

As we do not store any personal information on our servers, the risk of a personal data breach on our end is non-existent. However, we take reasonable security measures to ensure that the integrated AdMob SDK operates within safe parameters.

Your Rights

As we do not collect or store any personally identifiable information, the rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data do not directly apply to the data we hold. However, you have rights regarding the data that Google collects through AdMob. Please review the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy for information on exercising those rights.
