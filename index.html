Protecting your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how the "Personality Quizzes" application handles user data.

This information is governed by the Google AdMob privacy policy, which can be found at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Personal Information: We want to assure you that the "Personality Quizzes" application does not collect any personally identifiable information from you.

Quiz Results: The results of the personality quizzes are generated and processed locally on your device only. We do not access or store your quiz results on our servers.

Advertising Information (AdMob): This application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When advertisements are shown, AdMob may collect certain information about your device and your activity within the application. You can learn more about how Google uses this data in their Google Privacy Policy, which can be found at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Please be aware that AdMob may collect and share information in accordance with their own privacy policy, which can be found at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. We do not share any personal information with AdMob.

Data Security

As we do not store any personal information on our servers, the risk of personal data breach on our end is low. However, we take reasonable measures to protect the non-personal information collected by AdMob from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

However, you have rights regarding the data that Google collects through AdMob. Please review the Google Privacy Policy, which can be found at: https://policies.google.com/privacy for information on exercising those rights.
